# Character wardrobe review

Open the game, choose **Continue**, press **K**, then **Appearance**. New Expedition also opens the creator before beginning the journey.

- **Appearance:** six hairstyles (including new Trail braid, Tousled curls and High ponytail), hair/skin colors, tunic fabric and trousers. Save look changes appearance while keeping equipped items and belongings.
- **Wardrobe:** preview Trail leather, Fangbound bone and Skyshard crystal. Mix head, chest and legs independently; Current restores the actual outfit in the preview. These previews do not grant or equip items.
- **Turn preview** checks four directions. The motion button cycles idle, walk, run, axe, mining, sword, bow, fishing cast and petting poses. Held tool previews share the animation's hand anchors.
- To wear new armor in the world, craft its pieces at a **workbench** and equip through the existing Gear/inventory interface. The six new recipes use existing fangs, scales, fiber, crystal shards and prism crystals.

The real character, armor, hairstyles and tool actions share each rendered cel. Helmet coverage follows idle glances; scalp masks prevent hair poking through. Mounted characters retain their selected armor and appearance.
