# Fishing playtest

Fishing is integrated through the real ForestPlaytest selected-item right-click path. Equip the workbench-crafted Reedwood Fishing Rod, stand close to a silver ripple hole, and right-click it. Hold Space to raise the green cradle and release Space to lower it. Keeping the fish inside fills the catch bar. Escape, right-click, or the close button reels in without a reward or inventory cost.

The seeded forest has eighteen spaced shoreline holes and three distinct fish profiles. Reed Perch has slower movement and a broad cradle; Jade Shardfin is quicker with secondary movement; Moonscale darts with a smaller cradle. The first nearby hole offers the introductory Perch. Arbitrary water cannot be fished. Successful holes rest for ninety seconds, failed attempts for eight, and cancellations for two. Remaining cooldown and catch count serialize with the journey.

The minigame uses the original survivor sprite facing the hole, with a drawn reedwood rod, arcing cast, line, and bobber. Its side panel appears opposite the targeted hole to keep the character and line visible. Four original item sprites are generated by `tools/forest_fishing_icons.py`.

`ForestFishingTest.tscn` exercises actual physical keyboard and mouse events through Input, with renderer/OS pointer synchronization. Coverage includes:

- Only marked holes accept casts, with range and selected-rod requirements.
- Space held without balancing fails; a feedback-controlled sequence of actual Space press/releases wins each of the three fish.
- Every success awards exactly one fish; duplicate completion is ignored. Cancelling, losing, or dying gives no catch and consumes no rod.
- E/Q/number keys cannot leak into companion commands or hotbar selection. An external selected-item change cancels safely.
- An actual accepted hit interrupts fishing; an ignored invulnerable hit does not. Pause and death cancel fishing, and cooldown does not advance while paused.
- A full satchel leaves the won fish as a collectible on the bank. Freeing a slot picks it up exactly once.
- Restore produces the same holes and preserves catch count/cooldown. Settings remain byte-identical and test save writes are disabled.
- Selected-flask world right-click fills the exact occupied slot even with a full satchel, preserves source water, and rejects dry, distant, or wall-obstructed targets. Actual Satchel SlotUI right-click drinks a tonic at full hunger with damaged health, returns the flask to that same slot, preserves every other stack, and starts healing.

The final integrated suite passed **80 checks with zero failures headless and OpenGL-rendered**. Eight of those checks cover flask/tonic routing through the real world-input and inventory GUI paths rather than calling consumption functions directly. Final logs are in the parent playtest folder (`fishing-headless-log.txt`, `suite-fishing-pass5.log`). Live tracking captures cover all three species. No error or failure lines occur in either final log.

The fishing panel's input handler explicitly consumes numeric keys and wheel events. The initial focused HUD guard covered `_input`, but its numeric hotbar path lived in `_unhandled_input`; the additional input regression exposed and verified this fix.
