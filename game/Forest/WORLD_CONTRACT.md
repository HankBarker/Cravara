# Forest world contract

`ForestWorld.gd` is a self-contained, seeded 112 x 112 cell forest at 16 world pixels per cell. Instantiate under a y-sorted Node2D alongside the player. The world joins `forest_world` and also enables y sorting for its props. The ground is drawn by shaders (`ground/ForestGround.gd`: the baked land at z=-20, the live water surface at -19), built floors at -18, swaying grass and flowers (`ground/ForestFlora.gd`) at -17, other objects at normal y-sorted depth. After editing terrain, water or soil, call `surface.rebuild()` (the world does for its own edits).

- `get_spawn_position() -> Vector2`: safe origin.
- `get_spawnable_position(preferred: Vector2) -> Vector2`: nearby dry, unblocked creature spawn.
- `is_water_at(world_position) -> bool`: wading state for player/creature movement.
- `is_blocked_at(world_position) -> bool`: wall/tree/boulder/building or outside map.
- `mine_at(world_position, tool_type: String) -> bool`: accepts `axe` for trees, `pickaxe` for rocks/walls/crystals. Three hits for trees/walls, two for rocks. Wrong tool returns false; `last_feedback` explains it. Drops real ItemDB items through DroppedItem.tscn. The caller must enforce reach and attack cadence.
- `interact_at(world_position, item_id: String) -> bool`: gathers berry bushes and fiber ferns; exchanges `bucket` / `water_bucket` in the existing inventory slot; consumes and places `wood_wall`, `stone_wall`, `wood_door`, `stone_door`, `wood_floor`, `stone_floor`, `thatch_roof`, `slate_roof`, `campfire`, `workbench`, `torch`, `chest`, `hide_bed`, `tent` on vacant dry grid cells. Anything the keeper builds goes in `placed` (and `props[c].is_placed`) so it survives a save; code that builds for them must do the same. The caller must enforce reach and interaction cadence. Do not also consume successful actions in the caller.
- `get_interaction_hint(world_position) -> String`: hovered resource/landmark text.
- Points of interest: `pois` ([{name, kind, cell}], marked on the map), `lore_at` (cell -> `world/Lore.gd` id; E on that ruin calls the session's `show_lore`), signal `cache_opened(cell, loot)`, `is_dig_spot(pos)` / `dig_at(pos)` (relic mounds and wild roots, dug with the garden hoe via Gardening). `mine_at` refuses landmarks and caches ("cannot be dismantled") and dig spots ("Dig it up with a hoe."). Placement rules and save safety: skill reference `world-generation.md`, section 8.
- Houses: `is_sheltered_at(pos)` (a roof over that cell) and `is_roof_open(cell)` (the roof tiles joined to the one over the keeper, which fade so the keeper shows inside; the house next door keeps its roof). Which rooms count as homes is `folk/Housing.gd`'s job.
- The folk's places (`folk_hut`, `folk_camp`, `folk_cage`, `folk_cage_open`, in `Prop.FOLK_SITES`) are raised by `folk/FolkManager.gd`, not by the seed: `mine_at` refuses them ("This belongs to someone."). Brush cleared to make room for one is recorded in `mined`, so it stays gone through saves.
- `serialize() -> Dictionary`, `restore(Dictionary)`: seed, depleted prop coordinates (including dug relic mounds and roots), water edits, constructed props, chest contents, opened ancient caches (`caches`), and the kinds of floors and roofs (4th element; older saves load as timber and thatch). Save via root playtest controller. Chest and torch props wrap the existing scene as `PlacedObject`; chest preserves its `chests` group/proximity API for inventory UI integration.

Solid bodies use collision layer 16, included in the existing player's mask 22. Camp objects are static physical landmarks. The outermost cells cannot be mined. A natural ford east of spawn allows passage across the river. Water is an editable single-cell volume with animated surface and wading integration, not a pressure/flow simulation.

World updates CraftingManager's nearby stations within 64px every 0.4s. Initial workbench: (-56,-88); campfire: (56,-40). World tries player group first, then sibling `Player`.

## Validation

Run `res://Forest/ForestWorldTest.tscn` headless. Assertions cover dimensions, dry safe spawn, tool restrictions, repeated mining, exact modified-world serialization, bucket fill/empty, inventory exchange, placed wall collision and persisted construction, chest content restoration, and torch light existence. `res://Tests/WorldPOISuite.tscn` (headless) covers the points of interest: deterministic placement, the seeded props and old journeys untouched, caches, digging, landmarks, flora and flagstones, lore in the journal. `res://Tests/FolkSuite.tscn` (headless) covers stone building, the housing rules and the folk. The graphical fixture with user argument `--capture` writes `res://Forest/world_review.png`; `res://Tests/WorldLookCapture.tscn` (rendered) writes a look-book of the ground, water and every point of interest to `art/world-v2/`.

Art: native pixel tree/fern/rock regions are reused from the project's pre-existing `WorldObjects/Images/Objects.png`. Tribal tents, Sky-Fang shards, workbench, mineable banks and campfire are original code-drawn artwork using the project palette. Ground detail stamps, grass tufts and the ruins come from the craftpix packs in `assets_raw` (`tools/world/make_ground_art.py`, `make_poi_art.py`); point-of-interest item icons from the Raven Fantasy icon sheet (`tools/world/make_items.py`); the ground, water and flora shaders are original.
