# Leather and cloth interaction foley

Source: Kenney, RPG Audio 1.0, https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio
Downloaded 2026-09-15 from the official pack:
https://kenney.nl/media/pages/assets/rpg-audio/8e99002d76-1677590336/kenney_rpg-audio.zip

License: CC0 1.0. Original License-Kenney.txt accompanies these derived files. No attribution is required, but credit is retained here.

These are edits of recorded material handling sounds, not synthesized interface beeps:

- satchel-open: clothBelt + handleSmallLeather delayed 55ms; softened high frequencies and tail fade.
- satchel-close: dropLeather + cloth2 delayed 35ms; softened high frequencies and tail fade.
- equip-gear: beltHandle1 + clothBelt2; 0.55s tail trim/fade.
- unequip-gear: handleSmallLeather2 + cloth3; 0.44s tail trim/fade.

Mixed with FFmpeg, gain-reduced components and a 0.8 peak limiter, Ogg Vorbis quality 5. Runtime adds a quiet gain and slight pitch variation. Original source archive retained at art/forest-pass4/kenney-rpg-audio.zip.
